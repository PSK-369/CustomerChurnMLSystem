Part 5:
Step 4: Configure MLflow

Verify: $env:MLFLOW_TRACKING_URI

Step 5: Train the models
python training\eda_and_training.py

This script:

Loads customer data.
Cleans missing values and duplicates.
Encodes countries.
Creates date features.
Applies log1p to total_orders.
Trains Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, and Random Forest.
Logs metrics and models to MLflow.
Selects the best model.
Registers it as ChurnModel.
Promotes it to the Production stage.

end:
Registered ChurnModel  Production
